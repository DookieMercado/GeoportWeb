About Geoport Malaybalay

Geoport Malaybalay is a community powered mobile app designed to revolutionize road safety and response efficiency. We enable verified residents to report critical road defects potholes, cracks, vehicular accidents—instantly using their smartphone.

By focusing on verified, geo-tagged, and actionable reports, we've created a safety ecosystem where AI verifies, communities validate, and authorities respond fast. No bureaucratic delays. No guesswork. Just real time communication between residents and responders.

Every report doesn't just fix a single road it improves the entire city's resilience. Geoport is community safety at scale.
